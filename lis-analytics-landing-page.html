REMOTE-FIRST WHEN PRACTICAL

You may not need an on-site visit.

Depending on your equipment, network access, and documentation, LIS Analytics may be able to assess, configure, test, and commission much—or potentially all—of your monitoring solution remotely. That can reduce travel, installation time, and project cost.

Remote Assessment

Review existing PLCs, RTUs, radios, gateways, network architecture, and available data.

Remote Configuration

Configure gateways, Modbus mapping, dashboards, alarms, logging, and SMS notifications.

Remote Support

Securely troubleshoot software and communications without exposing critical control systems to the public internet.

LOCAL-FIRST ARCHITECTURE

Your monitoring system does not have to depend on the cloud.

LIS Monitor can run on an existing compatible Windows computer at the customer's facility. Live monitoring, historical logging, and alarm detection remain local, while optional internet services provide SMS, remote diagnostics, and off-site reporting.
